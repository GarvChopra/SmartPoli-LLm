# Maple Credit Union Help (fictional demo content)

## What should I do if my card is lost or stolen?
Freeze the card immediately, then order a replacement. Report stolen cards or unrecognized transactions to the fraud team. You are not liable for fraud reported within 60 days.

## How long does a replacement card take?
Standard replacement arrives in 5-7 business days and is free. Expedited replacement arrives in 2 business days for a $15 fee.

## What is the foreign transaction fee?
Maple Credit Union charges a 1% foreign transaction fee on purchases made outside the United States.

## What is the monthly account fee?
Checking accounts have no monthly fee when you have direct deposit. Otherwise the fee is $5 per month.

## What is the overdraft policy?
Overdrafts are covered up to $100 for a $20 fee per day. You can opt out of overdraft coverage at any time.

## How do I dispute a charge?
You can dispute a charge within 60 days of the statement date. Disputes over $500 are reviewed by a human specialist.

## What are the ATM withdrawal limits?
The daily ATM withdrawal limit is $600. Purchases with the debit card are limited to $3,000 per day.

## What is the wire transfer cutoff time?
Wires submitted before 3 PM Pacific time are sent the same business day.

## Will you ask for my PIN or full card number?
Never. Maple Credit Union staff and this assistant never ask for your PIN, password, or full card number. If someone does, it is phishing. Report it to the fraud team.

## What are your branch hours and contact details?
Branches are open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM Pacific. Demo phone: 555-0100. Demo email: demo@maplecu.example.
