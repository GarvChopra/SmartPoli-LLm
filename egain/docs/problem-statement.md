# Problem Statement: SmartPoli Care Navigator

## The Problem
Medication reminders only work when patients actually receive them and understand how to manage missed doses. Patients frequently experience notification issues (phone settings, Do Not Disturb, time-zone mismatches, or unconfirmed mobile numbers) and abandon reminder tools or call overloaded support lines, waiting on hold only to explain the same troubleshooting steps repeatedly. Support agents waste valuable time walking through basic device settings instead of resolving complex issues, while patients face frustration or risk taking inappropriate actions with their medication schedule.

## Intended Users
1. **Patients** using the fictional SmartPoli medication-reminder service who need help understanding features, troubleshooting missing reminders, or logging missed doses.
2. **Support Specialists** receiving structured, privacy-scrubbed escalation tickets with clear troubleshooting context.

## Business & Customer Value
- **Faster Resolution:** Resolves common reminder issues in under a minute via a guided 4-step check.
- **Zero Repeat Explaining:** When an issue isn't solved, the agent creates a structured support ticket (`SP-xxxx`) on the visitor's behalf, passing the category and scrubbed summary directly to support staff.
- **Safety by Construction:** Immediately halts normal workflows on urgent symptom keywords and directs visitors to emergency services (`911` / Poison Control `1-800-222-1222`), refusing to diagnose or advise on medication doses.
