# SmartPoli Service Overview
SmartPoli Care Navigator is a fictional medication-reminder service. It helps patients set up medication reminders, log missed doses, and reach a human support specialist.
SmartPoli is a customer-service tool. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or change medication. SmartPoli support staff are available Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Pacific time.
All features described here are part of a hackathon demonstration and are SIMULATED.
