# Reminder Troubleshooting
If reminders are missing, check in this order: (1) notifications are allowed for the SmartPoli app in phone settings, (2) Do Not Disturb is off or SmartPoli is allowed, (3) the phone time zone matches the time zone in the SmartPoli account.
Reminders can arrive up to 5 minutes late. If reminders are still missing after these three checks, create a support request.
Never skip or change a dose because a reminder did not arrive. Ask your pharmacist or care team.
