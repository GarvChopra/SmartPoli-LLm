# Privacy and Data Handling
SmartPoli collects the minimum information needed: member ID, medication names and schedules, and the contact preference.
In chat, never share passwords, full account numbers, or diagnoses. The demo scrubs email addresses and phone numbers from support request text.
Support request summaries passed to a human contain the category and a short description only.
Data in this hackathon demonstration is synthetic. No real patient data is stored.
