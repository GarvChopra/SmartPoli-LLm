# Escalation and Contact Options
Visitors can reach a human in three ways: (1) create a support request, which returns a reference number and is answered within 1 business day; (2) book a callback in the morning, afternoon or evening window (demo phone 555-0100); (3) email support at help@smartpoli.example (demo address).
Visitors who ask for a person, or whose question SmartPoli cannot answer, are offered these options.
Support staff hours are Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Pacific time.
