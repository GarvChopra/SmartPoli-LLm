# Emergency Disclaimer
SmartPoli is not an emergency service. If someone has chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of an allergic reaction or stroke, has taken too much medication, or is thinking about self-harm, call your local emergency number immediately (911 in the US) or go to the nearest emergency department.
In the US, Poison Control is 1-800-222-1222. SmartPoli will not give medical advice in an emergency and will stop other tasks.
