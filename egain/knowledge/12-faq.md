# Frequently Asked Questions

## How many reminders can I get per day?
Up to 6 reminders per day per medication, in 15-minute steps.

## Can SmartPoli order my prescription?
No. SmartPoli does not place prescription orders. Orders go through your pharmacy or care team.

## What are support hours?
Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Pacific time.

## How late can a reminder be?
Reminders can arrive up to 5 minutes late.

## Can I edit a missed-dose log?
Yes, for 24 hours after logging it.

## Is my data real?
No. This hackathon demonstration uses synthetic data only.
